A Beacon for the Arts

The Grand Palais Art Gallery is a digital museum dedicated to preserving and showcasing the masterpieces of the world's most influential artists.

Our mission is to democratize access to the wonders of art history, providing a high-quality, modern, and educational platform for everyone, everywhere. We curate a vast collection of public domain artworks, from the intricate sketches of Leonardo da Vinci to the vibrant brushstrokes of Van Gogh.

Our Collection

Over 1,000 public domain masterpieces archived and accessible for free.

Education First

Comprehensive biographies and context for every artist and artwork in our collection.
